# PTO Policy

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year during their first two years. This increases to 20 days after year 2 and 25 days after year 5.

## Requesting Time Off
Submit PTO requests through Workday at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 days. Emergencies can be handled by contacting your manager directly.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Additional unused days are forfeited on December 31.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 sick days per year, which do not carry over.
