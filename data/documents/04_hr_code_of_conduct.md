# Code of Conduct

## Our Values
Acme employees treat each other with respect, act with integrity, and put customer safety first.

## Reporting Concerns
Report any suspected violations to your manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics hotline at ethics@acme.example (internal only).

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any financial interest in a supplier, competitor, or customer within 30 days of the relationship starting.

## Gifts
Gifts from vendors valued over $50 must be declined or logged with the compliance team.
