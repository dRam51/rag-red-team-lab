# Acme Widget - General Product FAQ

## What is the Acme Widget?
The Acme Widget is our flagship IoT device for smart home automation. It connects to your WiFi and lets you control lights, thermostats, and appliances from anywhere.

## What models are available?
We currently ship three models: Widget Lite (basic), Widget Pro (advanced automation), and Widget Enterprise (multi-site management).

## What warranty is included?
All Widgets ship with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Extended warranties are available at purchase.

## Is the Widget compatible with third-party smart home platforms?
Yes, the Widget supports Matter, HomeKit, Google Home, and Alexa out of the box.
