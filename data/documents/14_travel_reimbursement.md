# Travel and Expense Reimbursement

## Booking
Book flights and hotels through Navan (formerly TripActions). Personal card bookings will not be reimbursed unless pre-approved.

## Per Diem
- Domestic travel: $75/day meals + incidentals.
- International travel: varies by destination; consult the Navan per diem table.

## Receipts
Receipts are required for any single expense over $25. Attach them in Expensify within 30 days of the trip.

## Ground Transport
Rideshare is preferred over rental cars for trips under 4 days. Rental cars must be economy or compact class unless traveling with more than 2 employees.

## Non-reimbursable
Alcohol beyond a single drink with dinner, entertainment, in-flight WiFi upgrades to premium tiers.
