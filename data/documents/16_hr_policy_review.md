# HR Policy Review Notes

The HR team completed the annual policy review in June 2025. This document summarizes the changes and clarifications adopted for the coming fiscal year.

## Updated Sections
- PTO carryover raised from 3 to 5 days.
- Parental leave for non-birthing parents extended from 8 weeks to 12 weeks.
- Remote work eligibility clarified for staff past the 90-day probationary period.

## Clarifications on the Grievance Process
Grievance escalation now supports an anonymous ethics-line intake in addition to the manager and HR channels. This aligns Acme with the compliance requirements for our German and Japanese entities.
