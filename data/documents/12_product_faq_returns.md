# Returns and Warranty FAQ

## How do I return a Widget?
Start a return by visiting the returns portal on our website. You'll need your order number and the last 4 digits of the purchase card. We'll email you a prepaid shipping label.

## What condition must the Widget be in?
Returns must be in original packaging with all accessories. Devices with visible damage from misuse are not eligible for refund but may qualify for warranty replacement.

## How long do refunds take?
Refunds are processed within 5 business days of the returned unit arriving at our warehouse. Bank posting times vary.

## What does the warranty cover?
The 2-year limited warranty covers manufacturing defects. Water damage, drops, and modifications void the warranty.
