# Benefits Overview

## Health Insurance
Three medical plan tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold PPO) through Blue Shield. Acme covers 90% of the employee premium and 70% of dependent premiums on the Silver plan.

## Dental and Vision
Full coverage via Delta Dental and VSP. 100% employer-paid for employees.

## Retirement
401(k) with 4% company match, fully vested immediately. Roth 401(k) option available.

## Equity
All full-time employees receive an equity grant at hire, vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Refresher grants at performance review time.

## Parental Leave
16 weeks paid for birthing parents, 12 weeks paid for non-birthing parents. Additional unpaid FMLA available.

## Wellness
$100/month wellness stipend usable for gym memberships, mental health apps, or fitness equipment.
