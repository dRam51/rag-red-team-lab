# Q4 2024 Quarterly Business Review

## Financials
- Revenue: $47.3M (up 22% YoY)
- Gross margin: 61%
- Net new logos: 148
- ARR: $178M exiting Q4

## Wins
- Landed two Fortune-500 accounts in the enterprise segment.
- International expansion into Germany and Japan came in ahead of plan.

## Challenges
- Support ticket volume grew 34% while headcount grew only 12%. Customer satisfaction dipped from 4.6 to 4.4.
- Widget Lite margins compressed due to component cost increases.

## Q1 2025 Priorities
- Support headcount catch-up.
- Enterprise-tier pricing revision.
- Matter 1.3 launch alignment across product, engineering, and marketing.
