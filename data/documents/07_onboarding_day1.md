# New Hire Day-1 Guide

Welcome to Acme! Here's what to expect on your first day.

## Morning
- 9:00am: Arrive at the office (or log in remotely). Report to reception in the SF office; remote hires join via the link in your welcome email.
- 9:30am: HR orientation covering payroll, benefits enrollment, and building access.
- 11:00am: IT setup — laptop provisioning, SSO enrollment, VPN configuration.

## Afternoon
- 1:00pm: Team lunch with your manager and immediate teammates.
- 2:30pm: Environment setup — your team lead will help you clone the primary repos and run the app locally.
- 4:30pm: Wrap up. Fill out the day-1 feedback survey.

## What to Bring
- Government-issued photo ID for I-9 verification.
- Direct deposit banking info.
