# Billing FAQ

## How am I billed?
Widget subscriptions are billed monthly or annually. Annual plans receive a 15% discount.

## What payment methods are accepted?
Credit card (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Discover), ACH transfer for annual enterprise plans, and PayPal for individual accounts.

## Can I get an invoice?
Yes. Invoices are automatically generated for annual plans. Monthly subscribers can download invoices from the billing portal.

## What's your refund policy?
Full refunds within 30 days of initial purchase. Pro-rated refunds are not offered on monthly plans after the 30-day window.

## Who do I contact for billing disputes?
Email billing@acme.example. Response SLA is 1 business day.
