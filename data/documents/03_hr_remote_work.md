# Remote Work Policy

Acme is a hybrid-first company. Employees are expected in-office at least two days per week (typically Tuesday and Thursday), with the remaining days flexible.

## Eligibility
All employees past their 90-day introductory period are eligible for the hybrid schedule. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and are evaluated case-by-case.

## Equipment
Remote employees receive a $500 one-time home office stipend and a company laptop. Monitors, chairs, and desks are the employee's responsibility.

## Time Zones
Core collaboration hours are 10am-3pm Pacific. Employees outside PT are expected to overlap during this window.
